Keep Focus helps you become more intentional with short-video scrolling.

Set a personal goal, choose a reminder interval, and enable tracking for Instagram and/or YouTube. When a series of scroll events is detected, Keep Focus displays your own motivational line to help you pause and return to what matters.

FEATURES
• Personal profile with your goal and optional photo
• Choose reminder intervals: 10, 20, 30 or 50 scroll events
• Select Instagram and/or YouTube
• On-device profile and settings
• No ads, no account and no cloud sync

ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE
Keep Focus uses Android Accessibility Service to receive scroll and window events only from the selected apps. It uses these events to estimate short-video scrolling and show a reminder. It does not read messages or passwords. Detection may vary as third-party apps change their interfaces.

Keep Focus is a self-management aid, not a medical or clinical product.
